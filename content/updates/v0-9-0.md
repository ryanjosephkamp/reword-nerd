reword_nerd 0.9 replaces the earlier site with a new app. The landing page is at the same
address, and the app is one click away. Old links, including `/image/` and `/preview/`, still
land in the right place.

## Hand it off in one paste

Pick where the work will run, and reword_nerd prepares exactly what that tool needs.

- **Coding agents** (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI): download a task folder and paste one
  command. The agent writes the result in the original format, Word files included.
- **Chat apps** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok): copy one prompt. **Go step by step** splits a
  long or delicate document into four prompts instead.
- **Image tools** (Grok Imagine, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney): attach the image and paste its
  prompt, or give Codex a folder of images to do in one go.

Folders and ZIP projects work in Text alongside single documents.

## Bring it back, if you like

Paste the reply or drop the agent's finished folder. You see the rewrite side by side with the
original or as changes in red and green, read the model's own check, and download the result in
the original format.

## Or skip the model

Edit the document right on the page, with headings, lists, bold, and italics. **Changes** shows
every edit the way a code review does. Choose a paper style (Paper, White, Sepia, or Night), a
serif or sans typeface, and a size, then download the document as Word, PDF, a web page,
Markdown, or plain text in that look.

## Around the app

- Five color palettes, each in light and dark, or following your device. The choice carries
  across the landing page, the app, and Updates.
- Side panels you can drag wider or narrower, or hide.
- A new quill logo.
- A [short scripted demo](../../#demo) on the landing page.
