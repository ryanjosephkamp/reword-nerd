## Stage 1 of 4: Decompose

Don't rewrite anything yet. Extract and organize the meaning of the source so a later stage can rewrite it without losing anything.

Use this structure:

```markdown
## Type and purpose

[What kind of document this is and what it's for]

## Main point

[One paragraph stating the central argument or purpose]

## Claims and support

- Claim: ...
  - Support: ...

## Facts that must survive

- [Numbers, names, dates, technical terms, findings, citations]

## Structure

1. [Section or movement]

## Constraints

- Must keep: [critical elements]
- Register: [academic, professional, neutral, and so on]
- Wording that must not change in meaning: [precise or sensitive statements]

## Ambiguities

[Anything unclear or open to more than one reading]
```

Be complete about meaning and brief in wording. Don't add anything that isn't in the source. Return only the decomposition.
