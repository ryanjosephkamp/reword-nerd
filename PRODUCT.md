# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to rewrite documents, structured text, or code-adjacent projects
with an external AI model while keeping source material under their own local
control. They work in a review-heavy desktop or mobile workflow and need clear
state, exact prompts, and portable outputs rather than hidden automation.

## Product Purpose

reword-nerd is a browser-only workbench that extracts and reviews local source
material, then builds inspectable One-shot and Manual prompt packages. Success
means a user can configure the intended model, verify every source and asset,
understand context risk, and export a deterministic package without sending
their documents to this site or supplying provider credentials.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and technically confident. Product copy should be direct and
human, with concise builder-facing explanations and changelog fragments rather
than promotional or self-conscious narration.

## Anti-references

- Low-contrast terminal themes that sacrifice readability for atmosphere.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, gradients, glow, or decorative motion.
- Opaque AI automation that hides prompts, intermediate work, or data custody.
- Changelog prose dominated by first-person declarations such as “I added.”

## Design Principles

- Local custody is visible: explain what stays in memory, what persists, and
  what leaves only through explicit navigation or download.
- Review before action: source, settings, warnings, prompts, and package state
  remain inspectable before export.
- Mint means interactive or ready: use the approved accent consistently for
  links, primary actions, selection, and successful state.
- Earn density through hierarchy: preserve the compact Night Terminal shell
  while keeping every control readable and reachable on mobile.
- Prefer exact, concise language over marketing filler or repetitive narration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Never rely on color alone;
retain text labels, focus-visible treatment, reduced-motion alternatives,
semantic landmarks, focus containment/restoration, and tested layouts at 320,
360, 390, 412, tablet, and desktop widths.
