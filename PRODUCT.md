# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who need to rewrite documents, structured text, or code-adjacent projects,
or prepare faithful reference-image prompts for an external image model, while
keeping source material under their own local control. They work in review-heavy
desktop or mobile workflows and need clear state, exact prompts, and portable
outputs rather than hidden automation.

## Product Purpose

reword-nerd is a browser-only workbench with an isolated Text default and Image
companion page. Text extracts and reviews local source material before building
inspectable One-shot and Manual prompt packages. Image prepares one revalidated
ZIP for the confirmed set, containing one revalidated reference-image
prompt/run-card pair per included image. Success means a user
can configure the intended model family, verify every source and setting, and
export a deterministic package without sending source material to this site or
supplying provider credentials.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and technically confident. Product copy should be direct and
human, with concise builder-facing explanations and changelog fragments rather
than promotional or self-conscious narration.

## Anti-references

- Low-contrast terminal themes that sacrifice readability for atmosphere.
- Generic SaaS dashboards, glassmorphism, gradients, glow, or decorative motion.
- Opaque AI automation that hides prompts, intermediate work, or data custody.
- Changelog prose dominated by first-person declarations such as “I added.”

## Design Principles

- Local custody is visible: explain what stays in memory, what persists, and
  what leaves only through an explicit download, clipboard copy, drag/open, or navigation action.
- Review before action: source, settings, warnings, prompts, and package state
  remain inspectable before export.
- Text teal means interactive or ready. Image orange means Image interaction or
  readiness, while its distinct yellow means review or pending attention.
  Neither portal relies on color alone.
- Earn density through hierarchy: preserve the compact Night Terminal shell
  while keeping every control readable and reachable on mobile.
- Prefer exact, concise language over marketing filler or repetitive narration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard behavior. Never rely on color alone;
retain text labels, focus-visible treatment, reduced-motion alternatives,
semantic landmarks, focus containment/restoration, and tested layouts at 320,
360, 390, 412, tablet, and desktop widths.
