How it works

Four steps, and the last one is a paste.

  1. Add

    Word, PDF, Markdown, HTML, LaTeX, code, whole folders and ZIP projects, or images. Scanned pages are read with OCR.

  2. Check

    See exactly what was read, fix it, and choose which files take part. Credentials and build folders are left out on their own.

  3. Set

    Pick a goal, tone, length, and language. For images, say what should change and what must stay.

  4. Hand off

    Copy one prompt, or download a task folder and paste one command. Bring the result back to compare and export, if you like.

The method

Decompose. Rewrite. Verify. Final.

Decompose

List every claim, fact, name, and constraint the rewrite must keep.

Rewrite

Write a new version in genuinely different words and structure.

Verify

Check for anything lost, added, or changed in meaning.

Final

Fix what the check found and finish the document.

Privacy

No server. No account. No upload.

reword_nerd is a web page that runs entirely in your browser. Files are read on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you copy it or download it.

Your model only sees what you paste or hand to your agent. Saving between visits is off until you turn it on, and then it stays in this browser.
